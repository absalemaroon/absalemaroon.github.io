Absalem Aroon is a Cybersecurity student at the Federal University of Technology, Babura, Nigeria, with a strong focus on distributed systems, blockchain technology, and cybersecurity research.

His interests center on understanding the underlying principles, architectures, and security properties of distributed computing systems, particularly blockchain networks and decentralized technologies. He is interested in how these systems are designed, how they achieve consensus, how cryptographic mechanisms support their operation, and how their security, reliability, and resilience can be evaluated.

His technical interests span distributed systems architecture, blockchain infrastructure, cryptography, decentralized protocols, and cybersecurity. He is particularly interested in the intersection of these fields, exploring the challenges involved in designing secure, reliable, and scalable distributed systems.

His approach emphasizes fundamental understanding, technical research, and practical experimentation. He aims to move beyond the surface-level use of technologies to investigate their internal mechanisms, design trade-offs, limitations, and potential applications.

He is currently developing his knowledge through independent study, technical exploration, and software experimentation, with a long-term interest in contributing to research and the development of secure and resilient distributed technologies.
